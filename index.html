Questions people ask first

How much does it cost to hire a food truck in London?

We quote a fixed price per head with a minimum spend, based on guest numbers, how long you want us serving and where the event is. Our food truck hire cost guide explains what moves the price, and the quote form gets you an exact figure within 24 hours.

Can we hire you for a party at home?

Yes, as long as the truck can get in and park safely — a driveway, a wide private road, a field or a hall car park all work. See private party food truck hire for what to check before you book.

Do you need power or water from us?

No. The truck carries its own, so you do not need to run cables out of the house or the venue.

How far ahead should we book?

Summer Saturdays and December go first. For those, a few months ahead is sensible. Midweek and off-season dates are often available at a couple of weeks' notice.

How it works

Four steps from enquiry to service

  1. Tell us the details

  2. Get a fixed quote

    We come back within 24 hours with a price per head, what is included and what we need on site.

  3. Lock the date

    A deposit secures the truck for your date. We handle the venue's paperwork requests directly.

  4. We turn up and cook

    We arrive early, set up in under an hour, serve your guests, then clear the pitch spotless.
