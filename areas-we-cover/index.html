Where we travel

Food truck hire across London and the South East

Based in London, we bring the truck to private parties, weddings and corporate events up to around 100 miles from base. Further afield is often possible — just ask.

Greater London

Food truck hire in London

We cover all 32 boroughs. The main thing that changes from one part of London to the next is where the truck can park.

North London

Barnet, Enfield, Haringey, Islington, Camden. Lots of house parties with driveways further out, and pubs, cricket clubs and halls closer in.

East London

Hackney, Tower Hamlets, Newham, Waltham Forest, Redbridge, Havering. Warehouse venues, courtyards and office car parks work well for parties and corporate events.

South London

Lambeth, Southwark, Lewisham, Greenwich, Croydon, Bromley. Plenty of gardens with side access and community halls with car parks.

West London

Ealing, Hounslow, Hillingdon, Hammersmith & Fulham, Richmond. Riverside venues, sports clubs and business parks near Heathrow.

Central London

The City, Westminster and Kensington & Chelsea. Very doable with a bit more notice: we check access, loading and parking for the exact address before we quote.

Not sure if we can park?

Send the postcode and a photo of the spot. Our private party guide covers driveways, street parking and council parking suspensions.

Home counties

Food truck hire outside London

Country venues, barns, marquees and big gardens are where a food truck really comes into its own.

Essex

Food van hire for barn weddings, marquee parties and corporate days from Brentwood and Chelmsford out to Colchester and Southend.

Kent

Garden parties, country house weddings and summer events from Dartford and Sevenoaks to Maidstone and Canterbury.

Surrey

Private parties and weddings around Guildford, Woking, Epsom, Reigate and the Surrey Hills venues.

Hertfordshire

St Albans, Watford, Hertford and the barn and estate venues across the county.

Berkshire & Buckinghamshire

Reading, Windsor, Slough, High Wycombe and Milton Keynes — corporate campuses and private estates alike.

Sussex

Weddings, private parties and summer events from Crawley and Horsham down to the coast.
