Why a truck in December

The Christmas party food people remember

The usual Christmas party food is a buffet that goes cold by nine o'clock. A food truck parked outside the office, the bar or the house does the opposite: hot food, cooked to order, served exactly when people want it.

Christmas party catering: quick answers

When should we book for a December date?

As early as you can. Thursdays and Fridays in the first three weeks of December are the most requested dates of the year. If you have a date in mind, ask us to hold it.

Can you serve in cold or wet weather?

Yes — we cook in all weather. The thing worth planning is where your guests will queue, so a spot near a covered entrance or a gazebo makes a big difference in December.

Can you invoice our company?

Yes. Corporate bookings get a written quote and a proper invoice, and we send insurance and hygiene paperwork straight to your facilities or venue team.

How much does a food truck cost for a Christmas party?

We quote a fixed price per head with a minimum spend, based on numbers, service time and location. December is peak season, so book early. Our food truck hire cost guide explains what moves the price.

Is your December date free?

Send the date, postcode and headcount. We will confirm availability and send a fixed quote within 24 hours.
