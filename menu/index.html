Important

Allergen information

Please speak to our team before you order. We can talk you through exactly what is in every dish, and we are happy to check ingredients with you at the hatch.

Our food is prepared in a single mobile kitchen where allergens including cereals containing gluten, milk, eggs, soya, mustard, sesame and sulphites are handled. We cannot guarantee any dish is free from traces of an allergen. If you have a severe allergy, tell us before you order and before your event so we can advise you properly.
