Documentation pack

We hold the standard trader documentation and send the full pack on request — usually the same day you ask for it.

  • Public liability insurance certificate
  • Employers' liability insurance certificate
  • Food Hygiene Rating 5, registered with Southwark Council
  • Gas safety certificate and appliance records
  • Electrical appliance testing records
  • Food safety management system (HACCP-based) and allergen matrix
  • Written risk assessment and method statement
  • Fire safety equipment and servicing records
