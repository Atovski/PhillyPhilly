How it works

Parties in London

Parking, access and permits

London parties come with London logistics. None of it is difficult, but it is worth knowing before you book.

  • Private land is simplest. A driveway, a garden with side access, a hall or club car park, or a field needs no extra permission.
  • Street parking needs planning. On a residential street or in a controlled parking zone, you may need a parking bay suspension from your borough council. Apply early — councils need notice.
  • Central London is fine, it just needs more notice. Tell us the postcode and we will flag anything that affects access or travel before you commit.
  • No space at home? Many pubs, sports clubs, community halls and private hire venues happily take a food truck. Ask the venue, then let us talk to them.

Three things worth checking first

  1. Can we get the truck in?

    Measure the narrowest point on the route and check for height barriers or low branches. Send us a photo if you are unsure — we would rather know now than on the day.

  2. Where will people queue?

    We need a bit of space in front of the hatch so a queue is not standing in a road or blocking a driveway.

  3. Is the council or venue involved?

    On private land you are usually fine. On a public road, or at a hired venue, check what they need before you confirm.

Private party food truck hire: your questions

How much does it cost to hire a food truck for a party in London?

We quote a fixed price per head with a minimum spend. The figure depends on guest numbers, how long you want us serving and the postcode. Send your date, postcode and numbers and you will have a written quote within 24 hours. Our food truck hire cost guide explains what moves the price.

Can a food truck park at my house for a party?

Often, yes. A driveway, a wide private road, a field or a hall car park all work, as long as the truck can get in and park on firm, level ground with space for a queue. Send us a photo of the spot and we will tell you honestly whether it works.

Do I need permission from the council?

On private land you usually do not. If the truck has to park on a public road, for example outside a terraced house, you may need a parking bay suspension from your London borough council. Check with them before you confirm the booking.

Do you need power or water from the house?

No. The truck is self-sufficient and carries its own power and water, so nothing has to be plugged into your house.

Can you cater for vegans, vegetarians and children?

Yes. Our Vegan Philly is fully plant-based — tell us numbers when you book — and the menu goes down well with children. Tell us about allergies well before the party so we can talk you through what is in each dish — see our allergen information.

How far in advance should I book?

Summer Saturdays and December book up first, so for those a few months ahead is sensible. Weekday and off-season dates are often available at a couple of weeks' notice.
