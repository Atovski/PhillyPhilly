Who we share it with

We do not sell your data and we do not add you to marketing lists without asking. We do use service providers who process data on our behalf: our website host, our enquiry form provider, our email provider, and — where you have consented — Google and Meta for analytics and advertising measurement. Some of these providers process data outside the UK; where they do, transfers are covered by the UK International Data Transfer Agreement or equivalent safeguards.

How long we keep it

Enquiries that do not lead to a booking are kept for up to 24 months so we can pick up the conversation if you come back to us. Booking records are kept for six years to meet accounting and tax requirements. Analytics data is retained according to the settings in the tools we use.
