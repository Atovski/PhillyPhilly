The food

Our menu

Thin-sliced steak, caramelised onions and molten cheese in a soft roll — plus the sides that keep the queue happy. Vegetarian options available on request.

Philly Cheesesteak

The one we are named after. Thin-sliced beef, caramelised onions and cheddar cheese sauce in a soft roll, served hot off the griddle.

New Orleans Chicken

Crisp outside, juicy inside, marinated in our own spice blend. Mildly spicy and aromatic — the pick for anyone who does not eat beef.

Philly Loaded Fries

Crisp fries buried under steak and melted cheddar sauce. Generous enough to share, though nobody ever does.

Mac & Cheese Croquettes

Golden fried on the outside, molten mac and cheese in the middle. Our most-ordered side.

Philly Cheesesteak Wrap

Same filling, softer format. Easier to eat standing up with a drink in the other hand.

How it works

Four steps from enquiry to service

  1. Tell us the details

    Date, postcode, rough guest numbers and what kind of event it is. Two minutes on the form.

  2. Get a fixed quote

    We come back within 24 hours with a price per head, what is included and what we need on site.

  3. Lock the date

    A deposit secures the truck for your date. We handle the venue's paperwork requests directly.

  4. We turn up and cook

    We arrive early, set up in under an hour, serve your guests, then clear the pitch spotless.
