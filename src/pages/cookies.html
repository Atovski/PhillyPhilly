Cookie policy

What we store on your device, and why.

Last updated: August 2026

What cookies are

Cookies are small files stored on your device by websites you visit. Some are needed for a site to work at all; others measure how the site is used or support advertising. Under UK law we have to ask before setting anything in that second group.

What we use

Cookie / storage Type Purpose
pp_consent Strictly necessary Remembers whether you accepted or rejected non-essential cookies, so we do not ask you again on every page.
Google Analytics (_ga, _ga_*) Analytics — consent required Tells us how many people visit, which pages they read and where they arrived from. Only set if you accept.
Google Ads (_gcl_*) Advertising — consent required Measures whether an advert led to an enquiry, so we do not waste money on adverts that do not work. Only set if you accept.
Meta pixel (_fbp) Advertising — consent required Measures results from our Facebook and Instagram advertising. Only set if you accept.

Changing your mind

You can withdraw or change your consent at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser settings, which will bring the cookie banner back on your next visit. You can also block or delete cookies entirely in your browser — the site will still work, we just will not be able to measure anything.
