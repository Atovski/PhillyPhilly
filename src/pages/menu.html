The food

Our menu

Everything is cooked to order on the griddle. Nothing sits under a heat lamp waiting for you.

Mains

Philly Cheesesteak

The classic, and the reason we exist. Thin-sliced beef seared on the griddle with caramelised onions, finished with cheddar cheese sauce in a soft roll.

New Orleans Chicken

Crisp on the outside, juicy inside, marinated in our own New Orleans spice blend. Mildly spicy and aromatic rather than fiery.

Philly Cheesesteak Wrap

Steak, caramelised onions and cheddar in a soft tortilla. The easier option if you are eating standing up with a drink in your other hand.

Sides

Philly Loaded Fries

Crisp fries buried under steak and melted cheddar sauce. Big enough to share, in theory.

Mac & Cheese Croquettes

Golden fried outside, molten mac and cheese inside. Our most-ordered side, by some distance.

Important

Allergen information

Please speak to our team before you order. We can talk you through exactly what is in every dish, and we are happy to check ingredients with you at the hatch.

Our food is prepared in a single mobile kitchen where allergens including cereals containing gluten, milk, eggs, soya, mustard, sesame and sulphites are handled. We cannot guarantee any dish is free from traces of an allergen. If you have a severe allergy, tell us before you order and before your event so we can advise you properly.
