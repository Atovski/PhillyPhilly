How much does it cost to hire a food truck in London?

The honest answer, what moves the price up or down, and how to get a quote you can actually budget against.

The short answer

Most food trucks price private events per head, with a minimum spend underneath. Across UK booking platforms, food van and food truck hire commonly lands somewhere around £8 to £25 per guest, with minimum spends that typically run from a few hundred pounds for a small midweek party to well over £1,000 for a peak-season Saturday.

Where your event sits in that range comes down to a handful of things, all of which you can see in advance.

What moves the price

1. Guest numbers

The per-head price usually drops as numbers rise, because the cost of getting the truck, staff and equipment to you is shared across more people. Below a certain size you will pay the minimum spend instead of the per-head rate.

2. How long you want us serving

A 90-minute evening food slot at a wedding or birthday costs less than an all-afternoon garden party or a full-day corporate event. Tell us the running order and we will price the window you actually need.

3. Where the event is

Travel within London and the home counties is part of the job. Central London postcodes can add parking, access and charging-zone costs, and events further out may carry a travel charge. We put these on the quote rather than surprising you with them later.

4. The date

Summer Saturdays (June to September) and December are the busiest dates of the year for every food truck in London. Midweek, Sunday and off-season dates are easier to get and often cheaper.

5. The menu

One main plus a side is the most efficient format. Adding more mains, sides or a choice for every guest increases prep and serving time, which shows up in the price.

What a food truck quote should include

Comparing quotes is only fair if they include the same things. Before you book anyone, check the quote says:

  • Price per head and the minimum spend
  • The service window (start and finish times)
  • What is on the menu and how many portions per guest
  • Travel, parking and any congestion or access costs
  • Whether the truck needs power or water from you
  • Deposit amount and when the balance is due
  • Insurance and food hygiene documents for your venue

Our quotes cover all of that in writing within 24 hours. Our truck is self-sufficient, so you will never see a line for hiring a generator.

Food truck vs a traditional caterer

For informal events — birthdays, garden parties, office socials, evening wedding food — a food truck is usually the simpler and often cheaper option. There is no venue kitchen to hire, no crockery, and no waiting staff. Guests queue, chat, and eat something cooked in front of them.

For a formal sit-down meal with courses served at the table, a traditional caterer is the better fit. Plenty of couples use both: a caterer for the wedding breakfast and a truck for evening wedding food.

Food truck hire cost: common questions

How much does it cost to hire a food truck for a party in London?

Most food trucks price private parties per head with a minimum spend. UK booking platforms commonly show roughly £8 to £25 per guest depending on cuisine, guest numbers and how long the truck serves. Central London, Saturdays in summer and December dates sit towards the top of that range.

What is a minimum spend?

A minimum spend is the lowest total a truck will accept for a booking, whatever the final guest count. It covers the fixed cost of bringing the truck, staff, fuel and set-up time to your event, so small parties usually pay the minimum rather than the per-head rate.

Is a food truck cheaper than a traditional caterer?

Often, for informal events. A self-sufficient truck brings its own kitchen, power and water, so there is no venue kitchen hire, crockery or waiting staff to pay for. For a formal plated meal a traditional caterer is the better fit.

Do food trucks charge for travel?

Most include travel within their usual area and add a charge for longer distances. Central London postcodes can also carry extra costs for parking and access. A good quote shows these up front.
