Before you book

Three things worth checking

  1. Can we get the truck in?

    Measure the narrowest point on the route and check for height barriers or low branches. Send us a photo if you are unsure — we would rather know now than on the day.

  2. Where will people queue?

    We need a bit of space in front of the hatch so a queue is not standing in a road or blocking a driveway.

  3. Are the neighbours or council involved?

    On private land you are usually fine. On a public road you may need permission from the council — worth a quick call to them before you confirm.
