Getting set up

What we need from your site

Site requirements for the Philly Philly food truck
Requirement What we need
Pitch A firm, level space with room to open the serving hatch and for a queue to form safely.
Access A route we can drive the truck down. Tell us about height barriers, bollards and gate widths in advance.
Power None required — the truck is self-sufficient. Mains hook-up is welcome if you have it.
Water None required. We carry our own.
Waste We take our own waste away unless you would rather we used site bins.
Set-up window Around two hours before service starts; under an hour of that is actual set-up.
Paperwork Public liability insurance, food hygiene certification, gas safety documentation and a risk assessment — sent to your team on request.
