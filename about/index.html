About us

About Philly Philly

A London food truck doing one thing properly: a Philly cheesesteak worth queueing for.

Our story

We started Philly Philly to bring a proper Philadelphia cheesesteak to UK streets and events — thin-sliced steak, caramelised onions and molten cheese in a soft roll, cooked to order on a hot griddle rather than assembled in advance.

Since then the truck has become a regular at street food markets, festivals and concerts, and we have catered weddings, corporate days and private parties across London and the South East. The menu is deliberately short. We would rather do six things properly than twenty things adequately.

Whatever the event, the approach is the same: turn up early, cook everything fresh, keep the queue moving, and leave the pitch cleaner than we found it.

The serious bit

Certification & compliance

Venues, councils and event organisers all ask for the same paperwork. We keep it current and send it over the same day it is requested.

  • Public liability insurance
  • Employers' liability insurance
  • Registered with Southwark Council — Food Hygiene Rating 5 (Very Good)
  • Level 2 Food Safety & Hygiene certification for catering staff
  • Gas safety certification for the mobile unit
  • HACCP-based food safety management system and allergen records
  • Written risk assessment and method statement for events
